Privacy Policy

QtMesh Games · Fernando Tonon · Effective 16 October 2026 (updated for optional QtMesh ID accounts)

In short: QtMesh Games has no ads or game-side tracking. Accounts are optional: if you sign in with a QtMesh ID, we store your profile, the logins you link and your cloud-saved game progress so it follows you across platforms. Without signing in, progress stays on your device. The website may use Google Analytics only after you accept a consent banner. When a game runs through Steam, Steam handles account and platform data under Valve’s policies.

Who we are

QtMesh Games is an independent collection of games by Fernando Tonon. This policy covers:

  • the website at games.qtmesh.dev;
  • games published under QtMesh Games on Steam and via the QtMesh Games desktop launcher;
  • browser builds of those games hosted on GitHub Pages (for example Scrapfall and Mall Chase web builds).

Individual titles may also publish a game-specific notice; if they do, that notice applies together with this policy.

Information we collect

If you don’t sign in, none for our own purposes. The games and the QtMesh Games launcher do not ask for, collect, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device advertising identifiers, or payment details to us.

If you choose to sign in with a QtMesh ID, we collect the information described in QtMesh ID accounts below.

QtMesh ID accounts (optional)

A QtMesh ID is one account shared by QtMesh Games, QtMesh Cloud and QtMeshEditor. You can sign in with Google, Discord, Steam, Roblox, GitHub, an email and password, or play as a guest. When you do, we store:

  • your display name, avatar and — when the login provider shares it — your email address;
  • the accounts you link (provider, account id and username), so any of them opens the same QtMesh ID;
  • cloud saves for games that support them: game progress, a short summary shown on your account page (such as level or best score), which platform saved it, and when;
  • a sign-in session token, kept in your browser’s local storage on this website.

We use this only to sign you in and to sync your progress between platforms. We never see your passwords for Google, Discord, Steam, Roblox or GitHub. Roblox game servers can read and update your saves only after you link your Roblox account, and never see your email. The data is hosted on Cloudflare as part of QtMesh Cloud; see the QtMesh Cloud Privacy Policy for retention and processing details.

Information stored on your device

To remember progress and preferences, a game may keep a small local save on your device, for example:

  • high scores, unlocks, and in-game achievements;
  • settings such as sound, display, or language.

That data stays on your device (or in your browser’s local storage for web builds) and is not sent to Fernando Tonon or QtMesh Games servers. You can remove it by uninstalling the game, deleting the game’s save folder, or clearing site data for a web build.

Steam and the QtMesh Games launcher

When you play through Steam, Valve provides the store, accounts, purchases, cloud features (if enabled for a title), and related services. That processing is governed by the Steam Privacy Policy and other Valve terms — not by this policy.

The QtMesh Games launcher may talk to a game over a local connection on your own computer to report achievements and game statistics. Those events may be forwarded to Steam through Steamworks when Steam is available. The launcher does not send that data to QtMesh Games servers.

Website and web games

  • games.qtmesh.dev is a static site. Signing in with a QtMesh ID is optional and happens on the QtMesh ID page at api.qtmesh.dev; otherwise the site has no forms that submit personal data to us.
  • Google Analytics 4: with your consent, the website loads Google Analytics (measurement ID G-35XP8NJK5Z, the same QtMesh stream used on qtmesh.dev) to understand aggregate traffic and which pages are visited. Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set until you accept the banner. You can decline; your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage. IP anonymization is enabled. Google’s processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Pages and game files may be delivered by GitHub Pages and related CDNs. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address in order to serve the files, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Browser games load only after you choose to play. The game itself does not send personal data back to us.

Roblox experiences

QtMesh Games titles on Roblox are played inside Roblox. Roblox provides accounts, networking, and platform services under the Roblox Privacy Policy. If you link your Roblox account to a QtMesh ID, our game servers use your Roblox user id to load and save your QtMesh progress. Otherwise we do not collect personal data for those experiences.

Third-party services

QtMesh Games titles do not embed third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs for our own use. The website may load Google Analytics after consent, as described above. Platform services you choose to use (Steam, Roblox, GitHub Pages, Google Play when a title is listed there) apply their own policies.

Children

Playing does not require an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country); QtMesh ID accounts are not intended for them. If you believe a child has created one, contact us and we will delete it. Platform age ratings and parental controls (Steam, Roblox, Google Play, and so on) still apply to each store listing.

Your choices

  • Use Steam’s privacy and account tools for Steam-related data.
  • Clear local saves or uninstall a game to remove on-device progress.
  • Clear browser site data for web builds.
  • Sign out, unlink logins or delete individual game saves from your QtMesh ID page; download or permanently delete your whole account from your QtMesh account settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
